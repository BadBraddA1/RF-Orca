# Product

## Register

product

## Users

RF coordinators and live-event crews after frequency coordination (Workbench / Coord). Coordinators create a show and share one link; crews mark what’s deployed on phones on the floor. Context is time-pressured, often on mobile, under stage lights — get in, mark, move on.

## Product Purpose

RF Orca is a lean, custom crew-facing alternative to SoundBase: after the plan is coordinated, share a mark board so crews can group channels and track deploy-by-room without accounts or spreadsheet handoffs. Success = create a show and share a link in under a minute; crews can mark deploy without scrolling past noise.

## Brand Personality

Lean · crew-first · signal-clear. Confident and practical, not flashy enterprise RF software. Nickname **Orca**; full name **Radio Frequency Orchestrator**.

## Anti-references

- Bloated RF / A/V SaaS dashboards that bury the task under nav and marketing
- Account walls and show libraries on the home page
- Overlong landing copy that pushes the “Create show” form below the fold
- Generic purple SaaS / cream-serif AI landing aesthetics

## Design Principles

1. **Task over theater** — Home exists to create a show; brand supports, never blocks.
2. **Phone-first floor use** — Mark view and create flow must work without hunting or zoom.
3. **Use only what you need** — Feature toggles; coordinators strip the board down for speed.
4. **Shareable truth** — One link is the show; no browsing past shows.
5. **Quiet power** — Coordinator tools stay subtle until unlocked.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA contrast on dark UI. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Touch targets ≥44px on mark view. Avoid relying on color alone for Allowed / Blocked / Deployed (labels + tags).
